Quick Start

Create your first dance plan

Follow these steps from top to bottom. You can reopen this guide from Help at any time.

  1. 1

    Load your rehearsal video

    Paste a YouTube link and select Load Video, or choose Open MP4 for a downloaded video.

  2. 2

    Set the music timing

    Enter the BPM and music start time below the video. Scrub the music map to find the exact moment you need.

  3. 3

    Prepare your dancers

    Add as many dancers as needed. Rename them in the roster, select a color from the numbered bubble, and choose a pose.

  4. 4

    Build each formation

    Play to the right moment, select Use Current Time, then drag dancers into place. Click dancers to select several at once.

  5. 5

    Map the transition

    Open the Map transition block between two cues. Drag the start and end bubbles, then add waypoints for curved or crossing paths.

  6. 6

    Preview and save

    Play the video to preview movement in 3D Plan. Use File > Save project to move the JSON file between desktop and phone.

Good to know Formation cues stay in timestamp order. Notes appear over the stage when their formation becomes active.
